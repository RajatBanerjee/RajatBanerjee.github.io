01. About

I'm a Senior Engineering Manager at Fanatics, leading the CRM and Content Supply Chain teams.

My expertise is in customer engagement at scale: CRM platforms, real-time customer data platforms, transactional email and SMS systems, and the content supply chain behind them — generation, audit, and metadata tagging. I believe the future of customer engagement is a single system where data, content, and delivery move together. Real-time personalization fails when the profile, the creative, and the channel are built in isolation.

I've been in software for 17 years — starting as an analyst at Dell, then DevOps consulting at Relevance Lab, rebuilding Art.com's product page, and 8+ years at Fanatics. I completed an MS in Cloud Computing at San José State in 2023, after a decade writing production code before moving into management.
